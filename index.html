Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpeedTracking ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the application.

Developer Contact
Email: ipmdn@icloud.com

Information We Collect

The App may access the following information and device features:

  • Location data (GPS)
  • Motion and sensor data
  • Vehicle speed measurements
  • App settings and locally stored session data

How Information Is Used

The collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Measure and calculate vehicle performance and speed
  • Improve accuracy and responsiveness of measurements
  • Store user sessions and preferences locally on the device
  • Provide app functionality requested by the user

Data Storage

All collected data is stored locally on the user's device unless explicitly stated otherwise in future app updates.

The App does not sell personal data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App currently does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Location Permission

Location access is required for speed calculation and performance measurements. Without location permission, some app features may not function correctly.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may contact the developer at any time regarding questions about privacy or requests related to your data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any future changes will be published on this page.
