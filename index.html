Privacy Policy

App: Speed Tracking  ·  Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Speed Tracking ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information we collect

The App accesses your device's GPS / location data solely to measure speed, distance, and movement metrics in real time. Specifically, we collect:

  • Precise location (GPS coordinates) while the App is in use
  • Speed and acceleration data derived from location updates
  • Session timestamps and duration

2. How we use your information

Location data is used exclusively to:

  • Calculate and display real-time speed and performance metrics
  • Store session history locally on your device for later review

We do not use your location for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core speed-tracking functionality.

3. Data storage and retention

All data collected by the App is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate servers, cloud services, or databases that receive your personal data. You can delete all stored sessions at any time from within the App, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your location or session data to any third party, including advertisers, analytics providers, or other companies.

5. Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking libraries. It uses only Apple's built-in Core Location framework to access GPS data.

6. Permissions

The App requests the "When In Use" location permission, which means location access is active only while you have the App open in the foreground. You can revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Speed Tracking. Revoking the permission will prevent the App from measuring speed.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
