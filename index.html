1. Summary

SpeedTracking is an iOS app for measuring vehicle performance metrics such as speed, acceleration times, distance, duration, average speed, and maximum speed.

Core privacy position: SpeedTracking does not require an account, does not use advertising identifiers, does not sell personal data, and does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Data we process

Depending on how you use the app, SpeedTracking may process the following categories of data on your device:

CATEGORY

EXAMPLES

PURPOSE

Location

GPS-derived speed, horizontal accuracy, speed accuracy, and location fix status.

To calculate and validate vehicle speed, distance, and acceleration-test results.

Motion

Device acceleration, gravity, rotation rate, and attitude from the device motion sensors.

To improve responsiveness and consistency of speed estimation and launch detection.

Test results

0–50, 0–90, 0–100 km/h times, max speed, average speed, distance, duration, and creation date.

To show run history and allow you to review previous measurements.

App settings

Sound preferences, thresholds, stabilization settings, calibration state, and onboarding state.

To remember your preferences and keep the app configured between sessions.

SpeedTracking does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, precise identity information, health information, contacts, photos, messages, or browsing history.

3. How we use data

SpeedTracking uses data only to provide the app’s core functionality:

  • calculating current speed and acceleration-test thresholds;
  • determining whether the vehicle is stationary, accelerating, or decelerating;
  • generating sound cues when configured thresholds are reached;
  • saving local run history and user settings;
  • improving measurement stability inside the app during a test session.

The app does not use your data for advertising, profiling, cross-app tracking, or resale.

4. Storage and retention

Run records and app settings are stored locally on your device using Apple platform storage. They remain on your device unless you delete them, uninstall the app, or reset the app data.

Location and motion samples are used during the active session to calculate speed and performance metrics. The app stores summarized run results, not a continuous public location trail.

Practical note: iOS backups or device-level services controlled by Apple may include app data depending on your device settings. Those services are governed by Apple’s own privacy terms and device settings.

5. Sharing, selling, and tracking

SpeedTracking does not sell personal data and does not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

The current app implementation does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies that follow you across other companies’ apps or websites.

If future versions introduce third-party services, cloud processing, analytics, crash reporting, or external integrations, this policy will be updated and the relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be changed accordingly.

6. Apple permissions used by the app

Location permission

SpeedTracking requests location access to obtain GPS-based speed and related accuracy information. Location access is needed for the main speed-measurement function. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Motion sensor access

SpeedTracking uses device motion data to support acceleration detection and speed-estimation stability. Motion data is processed locally during app use.

Sound output

The app may play sound cues when thresholds are reached if sound is enabled in the app settings. These cues do not require collection of audio data.

7. Your choices and rights

You remain in control of the app’s local data and permissions:

  • You can disable location access in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete saved run history from within the app where this function is available, or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can change sound and measurement preferences in the app settings.
  • You can contact the developer to ask questions about this policy or request support.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have rights under applicable data protection law, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability where applicable. Because the current app is designed around local-only processing and does not maintain a developer-side user account database, many requests can be handled directly by managing the data on your own device.

8. Children

SpeedTracking is not directed to children and is intended for users who can safely and lawfully use a vehicle-performance measurement app. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

SpeedTracking limits data handling by processing data locally where possible and avoiding unnecessary collection. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but reducing external transmission lowers exposure to third-party access.

You should use the app responsibly and avoid interacting with the device while driving. Measurements should be performed only in safe, lawful, and controlled conditions.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes, when legal requirements change, or when new features are introduced. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest version.
