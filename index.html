SpeedTracking

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 18, 2026

Summary

SpeedTracking is designed to process measurement data locally on your device. The app:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, or registration.
  • Does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • Does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.
  • Does not contact the developer's servers — there are no developer-operated servers.
  • Uses GPS and motion sensors strictly on-device to compute speed, distance, acceleration, and gate timing.

Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to the SpeedTracking mobile application ("the App") published on the Apple App Store. It explains how the App accesses device features, how locally stored data is handled, and the controls available to you.

Data the App Uses

The App accesses the following device capabilities only to provide its core functionality:

  • Location (GPS) — used during calibration and live test sessions to compute speed, distance, and gate times. Location is requested with the While Using the App authorization scope.
  • Motion sensors — accelerometer, gyroscope, and device attitude (via Core Motion) are used to detect vehicle launch, estimate longitudinal acceleration, and improve timing through sensor fusion.

Location and motion data are processed entirely on-device, in real time. They are not uploaded by the App and not retained beyond the local session/run data described below.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally, in the App's private sandbox, using on-device databases and files:

  • Saved run results — gate times, maximum speed, average speed, duration, distance, and quality estimates for runs you complete.
  • Run telemetry samples — time-series acceleration and speed used to render run charts and for later inspection.
  • Metrics traces and Live Session logs — diagnostic logs generated by the App when logging is enabled.
  • Vehicle profiles — optional textual information you enter (brand, model, type, year) and the vehicle associated with each saved run.
  • Calibration data — calibration direction, quality score, sensor bias, noise floor, and the calibration trace.
  • App settings — gate targets, stabilization parameters, sound preferences, and onboarding acceptance status.

No account, sign-in, or cloud sync is required. Locally stored data remains on your device unless you choose to export a backup or share a log manually.

What the App Does Not Collect

  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) is requested.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera, calendar, reminders, or HealthKit data is accessed.
  • No browsing history, device identifiers, or telemetry is collected for the developer.
  • No data is sent to third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Backup Export and Import

SpeedTracking includes optional Backup Export and Backup Import features. When you export a backup, the App creates a single JSON file containing local app data such as settings, vehicle profiles, saved runs, telemetry, metrics traces, and the Live Session log.

Backup export and import are initiated only by you, through the App's user interface. Exported backup files may leave the App sandbox depending on where you choose to save or share them (e.g., iCloud Drive, Files, AirDrop, email, or another destination selected via the system file picker).

You are responsible for storing, sharing, and deleting exported backup files outside the App. The developer has no access to these files.

Deleting Your Data

You can remove locally stored data at any time from inside the App:

  • Settings → History and Metrics Logs → Delete History / Metrics Logs: removes saved runs and/or run-chart metrics traces.
  • Settings → Data → Delete saved data: removes saved runs, telemetry, logs, vehicle profiles, calibration data, and resets app settings on the device (onboarding acceptance is preserved).
  • Delete the App: uninstalling SpeedTracking from your device removes all app-private storage created by the App.

Deleting data inside the App does not automatically delete backup files you previously exported outside the App.

Permissions and System Controls

iOS asks for your explicit permission the first time the App needs access to Location or Motion data. You can review and change these permissions at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security and in Settings → SpeedTracking.

If Location permission is denied or restricted, GPS-dependent features (live tests, speed/distance calculation) will not function. The App will surface a notice when permission is required.

Third-Party Services

SpeedTracking does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, attribution services, or crash-reporting providers. The App does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties because no personal data is collected by the developer.

Children's Privacy

SpeedTracking is not directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact the developer so any concerns can be addressed.

Safety and Measurement Accuracy

SpeedTracking is intended for indicative measurements only. It is not a professional telemetry tool and should not be used as an official reference. Use the App responsibly, only in safe conditions, and in compliance with local laws and traffic regulations. The phone must be firmly mounted in the vehicle and must not be held in hand while driving. Do not operate the App in a way that distracts you while driving or compromises safety.

Security

Locally stored data is kept within the App's sandbox using standard iOS data protection. Because the App does not transmit your data to remote servers, the primary safeguards are those provided by the iOS operating system (device passcode, system encryption, and per-app sandboxing).

Your Rights

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer, there is no remote profile, account, or remote dataset to access, correct, or delete. You can exercise full control over your locally stored data through the in-app deletion controls described above. For questions related to privacy, contact the developer using the address below.
